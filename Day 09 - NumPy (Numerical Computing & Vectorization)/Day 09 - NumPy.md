## Day 09: NumPy (Numerical Computing & Vectorization)

NumPy (Numerical Python) is the foundation of the modern Machine Learning, Data Science, and AI ecosystem. It replaces slow Python lists with blazing-fast, C-optimized multi-dimensional arrays (`ndarrays`).
